# Adversarial pass on analyst spec — 2026-05-25

## Summary

The spec gives an implementer a fair map of the three wiring gaps but
leans on a load-bearing wrong premise (it partitions clauses by
`MinDepthTier > DepthRankNone` rather than by `DepthType ==
DepthTypeSensitive`), confuses two unrelated senses of "universal"
(language-bound vs. auto-applied-to-every-arrow), and leaves the
amendment-drain firing rule, OperatorBus payload contracts, dispatcher
recursion, and registry coverage check materially underspecified.
Roughly half of the §11 invariants the spec references are silently
unenforceable as written; the rest are correct.

## Adversarial verdict

The spec is structurally salvageable — the four-section layout (firing
rule / inputs / outputs / acceptance / termination) is the right
shape, and most of its prose is operationally accurate. But it carries
**one wrong premise (C1) that invalidates the partitioning of arrows**,
**one terminology conflation (C2) that breaks the Gap-3 table**,
**one API-shape mismatch (C5) that means the spec's mandated loop
composition does not type-check in Go**, **two undocumented orchestrator
duplications (C6, H8)**, and **multiple data-shape gaps (H4, M3) that
the implementer cannot resolve without a follow-up architect spec.**

Recommendation: send back to analyst for one revision pass focused on
(i) replacing `MinDepthTier > NONE` with `DepthType == DepthTypeSensitive`,
